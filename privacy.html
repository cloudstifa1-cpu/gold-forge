Ore to Alloy Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Ore to Alloy is a free study app. It does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal information.

  • No accounts, sign-ups, or logins.
  • No ads, analytics, or tracking.
  • Settings like text size, saved topics, and your last search are stored only on your own device (browser local storage). They never leave your device, and clearing your browser data deletes them.
  • The app loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which receives a normal web request (such as your IP address) when fonts download.

The app is free for anyone and is suitable for all ages.

Questions: open an issue at github.com/cloudstifa1-cpu/gold-forge/issues.
